# SOP: Approval Process

> SYNTHETIC internal document of a fictional agency, written for the CreatorOps Copilot project.

## 1. What needs approval
1.1 Every scheduled post, every change to a scheduled post and every deletion needs approval.
1.2 Sponsored posts need approval from the account manager AND, if the contract says so, from the brand.

## 2. Who approves
2.1 Organic posts: the account manager.
2.2 Sponsored posts: the account manager, then the brand contact if brand review is required by contract.
2.3 Restricted categories (alcohol, supplements, financial products): the team lead in addition.

## 3. Disclosure checklist for sponsored posts
3.1 The platform's paid partnership / branded content label is switched on.
3.2 The caption starts with a clear disclosure such as "#ad" or "Paid partnership with [Brand]". A disclosure hidden at the end of the caption or among many hashtags is not accepted.
3.3 For video content the disclosure is also said or shown in the video.
3.4 The brand is tagged as the business partner.
3.5 For alcohol brands the age restriction is set on the post.

## 4. Recording decisions
4.1 Every approval or rejection is recorded with the name of the person, the date and a reason for rejections.
4.2 Nothing is published without a recorded approval. This also applies to actions proposed by the AI assistant.
