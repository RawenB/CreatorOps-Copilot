# SOP: Brand Contract Review

> SYNTHETIC internal document of a fictional agency, written for the CreatorOps Copilot project.

## 1. Clauses to check every time
1.1 Term and renewal: start and end dates, automatic renewal.
1.2 Exclusivity and non-compete: which categories, how long after the end of the term.
1.3 Usage rights: how long the brand can reuse the content, and whether paid ads (whitelisting) are included.
1.4 Payment: amount, split, payment delay (net days), late fees.
1.5 Termination: notice period, kill fee, morality clause.
1.6 Governing law.

## 2. Red flags
2.1 Perpetual or unlimited usage rights.
2.2 Exclusivity covering a whole industry for more than 6 months after the term.
2.3 Payment more than 60 days after delivery.
2.4 Any clause asking the creator not to disclose the partnership. This is never acceptable.

## 3. Conflicts
3.1 Before signing, check the creator's running contracts for exclusivity in the same category.
