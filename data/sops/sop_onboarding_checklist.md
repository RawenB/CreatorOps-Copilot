# SOP: Creator Onboarding Checklist

> SYNTHETIC internal document of a fictional agency, written for the CreatorOps Copilot project.

## 1. Contract and admin
1.1 Signed talent management agreement stored in the shared drive.
1.2 Invoicing details and tax information collected.
1.3 List of existing brand deals and any exclusivity clauses still running.

## 2. Accounts
2.1 Switch all accounts to a creator or business account.
2.2 Add the agency as a partner in Meta Business Suite and TikTok Business Center (never ask for passwords).
2.3 Turn on two-factor authentication on every account.

## 3. Brand safety profile
3.1 Topics the creator does not want to promote (for example alcohol or gambling).
3.2 Brand categories that conflict with current exclusivity clauses.

## 4. First month
4.1 Baseline analytics report for the last 90 days.
4.2 Content calendar for the first month.
4.3 Short training on disclosure rules for sponsored content (FTC, ASA/CAP, platform tools).
