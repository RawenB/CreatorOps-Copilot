# SOP: Posting Workflow

> SYNTHETIC internal document of a fictional agency, written for the CreatorOps Copilot project.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure describes how a post goes from idea to publication for every creator we manage.

## 2. Content calendar
2.1 Each creator has a monthly content calendar prepared by the account manager before the 25th of the previous month.
2.2 The calendar lists the platform, format (Reel, Story, carousel, TikTok video, YouTube Short), topic and whether the post is sponsored.

## 3. Drafting
3.1 The creator sends a draft (video or photo + caption) at least 3 business days before the planned date.
3.2 For sponsored posts the draft is also sent to the brand if the contract requires brand review.

## 4. Checks before scheduling
4.1 The account manager checks the caption for spelling, links and hashtags.
4.2 For sponsored posts the account manager runs the disclosure checklist (see SOP: Approval Process, section 3).
4.3 Posts for alcohol, supplements or gambling-related brands need a second check by the team lead.

## 5. Scheduling
5.1 Posts are scheduled in the agency scheduling tool only after approval is recorded.
5.2 Default posting times: weekdays 18:00-20:00 local time, weekends 11:00-13:00, unless analytics show a better slot for the creator.

## 6. After publication
6.1 The account manager checks the live post within 1 hour (label visible, link working, tags correct).
6.2 Metrics are pulled after 24 hours and after 7 days for the campaign report.
