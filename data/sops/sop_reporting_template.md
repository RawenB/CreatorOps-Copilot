# SOP: Campaign Reporting Template

> SYNTHETIC internal document of a fictional agency, written for the CreatorOps Copilot project.

## 1. When
1.1 A campaign report is sent to the brand 10 days after the last deliverable.

## 2. Metrics to include
2.1 Reach, impressions, views (for video formats), likes, comments, shares and saves per deliverable.
2.2 Engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares + saves) / reach.
2.3 Comparison with the creator's average for the same format over the last 90 days.

## 3. How to read the numbers
3.1 Stories are compared with Stories only, Reels with Reels, because reach differs a lot between formats.
3.2 A sudden jump in likes without a jump in reach is a sign of fake engagement and must be flagged, not reported as a success.

## 4. Structure
4.1 Summary (3-5 lines), results table, best performing content, learnings, next steps.
