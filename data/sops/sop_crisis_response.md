# SOP: Crisis Response

> SYNTHETIC internal document of a fictional agency, written for the CreatorOps Copilot project.

## 1. What is a crisis
1.1 A post that receives a large wave of negative comments, a platform strike or removal, a brand complaint, or a hacked account.

## 2. First hour
2.1 The account manager informs the team lead and the creator.
2.2 Scheduled posts for the creator are paused.
2.3 No public reply is posted before the team lead approves it.

## 3. Platform strikes and removed content
3.1 Read the notification and identify the exact policy mentioned.
3.2 If the removal looks wrong, submit an appeal through the platform within 24 hours.
3.3 Record the strike in the creator file, with the policy and the date.

## 4. Brand complaints
4.1 Check the contract (deliverables, disclosure, morality clause).
4.2 Answer the brand within one business day with the facts and a plan.

## 5. After the crisis
5.1 Write a short report: what happened, what we did, what we change.
